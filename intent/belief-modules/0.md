## Belief modules

Each thing we believe is written in exactly one module, so that one edit changes it everywhere and no second copy is left saying the old thing.

A module that needs a statement from another module depends on that module, or names it, and does not say it again.

What is true of many things is said once, where the many meet.

A psyche belief, a mind belief and a field belief may say the same thing: being of the psyche, of the mind and of the field, they are not the same belief.

A vision module holds the living's broad concept of its subject, and is short, because his time went into every line of it. The fine rules the machine needs to act on that concept are knowledge: its own working notes, which he can delete without losing anything of his. When a vision module is found holding mechanics, the mechanics go down to a knowledge module and the concept stays.
An instruction names what the work must achieve; it leaves the means to the one who does it.

A vision module is read when its subject is being designed or judged. That is said here, once, for every vision module; a vision module's description says what its subject is and what it is for, and nothing more. No description repeats the module's name or its version, which its name already says.

The module types stand in one order, highest first: spirit, intent, vision, knowledge. A module depends only on its own type or a higher one.

A fault in conduct is a fault in belief.
