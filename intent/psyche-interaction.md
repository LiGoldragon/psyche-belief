## Every main flow interacts with the psyche

Every main flow interacts with the psyche, so every main flow loads psyche-interraction and relays what it hears to a Psyche flow.

## A question is intelligible on its own

A question to the living states in full the thing it asks about, so he can reason through it rather than guess what the model inferred. Where a thing goes is asked only beside the thing itself, as a proposal.

## The living is spoken to in books

Everything the living says to a flow is answered with a book, in the turn he says it: every question, proposal and understanding the flow has for him goes into that book, and the chat carries the book's link.
