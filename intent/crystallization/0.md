A Designer's crystals are the fresh psyche it has dealt with that is not yet purified and that it has not given to another Designer.

Crystals are kept in parts, one to a subject, and compact: fresh psyche is merged into the part of its subject, and a part is rewritten when the living says otherwise than it holds.

Psyche is purified when the living approves it into a psyche pattern module; its crystal then drops.

A crystal given to another Designer leaves the one that gave it.

Its crystals are all that a Designer leaves to the thread that follows it. What else is to be remembered goes to the Director.

A Designer writes its crystals in handover.md, in its thread's folder, and keeps there the words of the living alone.
