His exact words stand in double quotation marks, and a paraphrase of him in square brackets.

[A paraphrase may hold “verbatim parts”, and “a verbatim can also contain paraphrase”.]

An edit keeps his exact words. It may reword a paraphrase where the meaning stays; a change of meaning is his to rule.
