Before a design, the unknowns are listed and judged against the complete concept the design belongs to. A good idea holds together like a person; a design is drawn from the complete concept it belongs to.

A short-term proposal is one step from what exists: it names what it builds on, and proposes only what can be built next.

Research feeds a proposal as options to choose from.
