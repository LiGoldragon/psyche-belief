## Context modules

A context module is dense and precise: the fewest words for the largest effect, spirit and intent most of all. A statement lives in one module only.
An instruction names what the work must achieve; it leaves the means to the one who does it.
A module named kind:name reads as its kind before the colon and its name after it: vision:book is the module book, of the kind vision.
