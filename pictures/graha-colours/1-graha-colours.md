# Picture 1: the nine grahas in colour (file: graha-colours)

One sheet, nine rows, one per graha, top to bottom: Sūrya, Candra, Maṅgala, Budha, Guru, Śukra, Śani, Rāhu, Ketu. At the left of each row the graha's name (IAST, with English: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rāhu, Ketu) and its glyph. Then a row of large round swatches, each filled with the colour and named beneath by the word the text uses and the thing it is compared to. The first swatch of each row is larger: the colour most sources agree on.

- Sūrya: rakta, red (vermilion) #D8341E, larger; tāmra, copper #B5622E; hiraṇmaya, golden #E2A93B; padmagarbha, lotus-heart pollen #F2C14E; raktaśyāma, red-dark #7A2A1E.
- Candra: śveta, white (kunda jasmine) #F7F5EE, larger, with a thin ink ring; sphaṭika, crystal #E6EEF2; rajata, silver #C9CED6; kumuda, white water-lily #F3F0E4.
- Maṅgala: rakta, blood-red #A8131B, larger; aṅgāraka, live coal #E0461E; kiṃśuka, flame-of-the-forest flower #E8702A; pravāla, coral #E0614F; tapta-tāmra, heated copper #C2562B.
- Budha: dūrvā-śyāma, dark green of dūrvā grass #3F6B2A, larger; śuka, parrot green #3FA34D; karṇikāra, karṇikāra-flower yellow #F2C230; hema, gold #D9A53A.
- Guru: pīta, yellow (heated gold) #E3A81F, larger; gaura, fair-golden #E8CF95; kumuda-sphaṭika, lily-and-crystal white of the good Jupiter in the sky #F4F2EA; puṣparāga, topaz #E9B949.
- Śukra: śveta, white #FAF8F2, larger, thin ink ring; citra, variegated: a swatch split in four wedges of rose #D9738A, green #5E9E5A, gold #D8A93F, sky blue #8DB8DE; dūrvā-dark #4C6B3A; rajata, silver #C9CED6.
- Śani: kṛṣṇa, black #1A1A1F, larger; indranīla, sapphire blue #1F3A7A; vaiḍūrya, cat's-eye blue-green #3E6E6A; atasī, flax-flower blue #5B7FC2.
- Rāhu: nīla, deep blue-black #1C2340, larger; dhūmra, smoky #6E6A66; kṛṣṇa, black #1A1A1F.
- Ketu: dhūmra, smoky #8A847D, larger; citra, variegated (four wedges of smoke #8A847D, flame #D8541E, black #1A1A1F, pale #D8D4CC); agni, fire #D8541E.

Sources line: Bṛhajjātaka 2; Bṛhat Saṃhitā 3–11; Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra 3, 84; Matsya Purāṇa 93–94; Viṣṇudharmottara III 67–69; Sārāvalī 4; Jātakapārijāta 2; Phaladīpikā 2; Yavanajātaka 1.120.
