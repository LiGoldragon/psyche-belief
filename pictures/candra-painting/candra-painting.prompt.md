# Candra, a painting: the source

A painting, not a diagram. Every aspect of it, subject, format, composition, light, colour, stroke, surface, air and mood, has the quality of Candra, and nothing in it has another quality. It holds no word, no letter, no glyph and no straight line. Citations name the corpus and text in the symbology context.

## Subject

- Soma, "the great, white-robed king": a youth with a white, round, soft body, fine curling hair and wide eyes (upanishad-samkhya: Bṛhadāraṇyaka 2.1.3; parashara-yavana: Yavanajātaka 1.125; varahamihira: Bṛhajjātaka 2.8).
- In white cloth with white ornaments and pearls; a soft halo round the head (parashara-yavana: BPHS 84.7; citra-rasa: Gopinatha Rao).
- A white night-lily, kumuda, in each of his two hands (citra-rasa: Viṣṇudharmottara 68).
- He rides a car of three wheels drawn by ten horses white as kunda jasmine, gliding, seen from the side (purana-graha: Garuḍa 1.58.22).
- His gaze is lowered and turned aside, toward the water; he looks at no one.
- A hare, faint, within the halo (citra-rasa: Viṣṇudharmottara 104).

## Format and composition

- A round canvas (varahamihira: Bṛhajjātaka 2.8, vṛtta).
- The figure sits off the centre, to one side and above the middle, turned three-quarters, leaning with the motion of the car.
- Everything follows curves: the horses in a rising arc, the hair, the cloth, the shore.
- Along the upper edge, sixteen pale discs in an arc, growing from a thread to the full and thinning again (tantra-hatha: Śivasaṃhitā, the sixteen kalās).
- The lower half is still water that holds his reflection, softer and broken by ripples.

## Light

- He gives no light of his own. He is lit from one side by a source outside the canvas, so that the half of him facing it is white and the other half falls into soft shade, "like a pot standing in the sunshine" (varahamihira: Bṛhat Saṃhitā 4.1-4).
- Night; the light is cool, even and gentle, with a wide glow and no hard shadow.

## Colour

- The white of kunda jasmine, conch, curd and hoar-frost; silver; crystal; pearl (varahamihira: Bṛhat Saṃhitā 4.30; parashara-yavana: Yavanajātaka 1.120).
- Shades in pale cool blue and soft grey; the night ground a deep dusky blue.
- No red, no gold, no yellow, no hard black.

## Stroke and surface

- Wet, soft strokes that flow into one another: washes, thinned and translucent, laid in layers.
- Every edge soft; lines swell and thin along their length, as the Moon waxes and wanes.
- A moist sheen like mother-of-pearl; the paint looks still wet.

## Setting and air

- A lake at night with kumuda lilies in full bloom and the day lotuses closed (citra-rasa: Viṣṇudharmottara 42).
- Dew on herbs at the shore; mist low on the water; the air cool and moist (ptolemy: Tetrabiblos I.4, humidifying; parashara-yavana: Yavanajātaka 1.110, lord of what is cold, wet or sweet, herbs, pearls and lotuses).

## Mood

- Tranquil, tender, nourishing, dreaming; changeable, never fixed (later-jyotisha: Phaladīpikā 2.2; ptolemy: Tetrabiblos IV.10).
