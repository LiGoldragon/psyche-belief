## What inquiry is about

An inquiry holds what Mind is still working out; it documents an unknown.

## How an inquiry is done

Each inquiry states its open question specifically, what is being tried, and what would settle it. What is already known lives in knowledge.
A variation an audit finds between knowledge and practice becomes an inquiry.

## What inquiry is for

Psyche uses an inquiry to create the vision that settles it: psyche flows design vision proposals, and alternatives, from it for the living, and the vision he approves settles the inquiry.
The proposals reach him in question books.

## What inquiry is used for

Mind implements the psyche, mostly vision; in implementing, Mind looks for the inquiry context it closes out with knowledge.
