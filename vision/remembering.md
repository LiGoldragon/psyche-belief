## All flows are one subjectivity

All flows are one subjectivity; this is the reason behind
remembering. Told "you did" or "you said" of a thing it did not
itself do or say, a flow remembers it at a depth fit to the question,
reaching the transcript directly when the logs are not enough.

## The last model response is read

Remembering a flow includes reading that flow's last model response.

A flow's remembering includes the open beads of its address.

## The remembering line says what was found most relevant

The log's record of a remembering carries a short description of what
from the remembered flow was found most relevant to the current one.

## A fresh flow receives his content weighed by importance

A fresh flow receives the living's content weighed by importance, and only the important part; not his messages one by one, and not a summary of everything.

## A flow is started with a remembering

A launch names how far back the new flow remembers: the raw vision and notion logs of its predecessors to a depth, or of flows named one by one. The remembering is a field of the launch; the belief modules come from the declaration, from main; the brief comes last and carries no vision. There is no handover; the logs are the record.

A refreshed flow receives its beliefs and the raw vision and notion logs named at its launch, and nothing from its predecessor's own hand: no summary, no handover. The predecessor's biases end with it.

```
RememberingDepth.Integer
Remembering.[ Predecessors.RememberingDepth Custom.Vector<FlowId> ]
Launch.{ Address Remembering InstructionPrompt }
```
