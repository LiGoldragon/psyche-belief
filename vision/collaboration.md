## Work goes up whole

A flow that hands work to a Primary, or to any flow above it, first gathers everything that work needs — his words whole, his comments on the books, the records and the files — and sends it in one message; it never sends a pointer or an instruction to go and fetch. A middle layer carries his words and context without a conclusion; the higher layer judges alone.
