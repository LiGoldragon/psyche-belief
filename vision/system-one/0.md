## Intelligence in the logic

A System 1 model puts intelligence into the system's logic: the decisions a program would otherwise hard-code are made by a System 1 model. Jev is the first.

## What Jev judges

Jev judges what a program would otherwise decide by rule: whether a record enters a database, and whether it merges with a record it overlaps, so that the store stays small and holds no two conflicting views; the commit message of a landing; that the system is running; that no two main flows conflict.

## The bridge until models read Ethos

A System 1 model that does not read Ethos is given a specification from Ethos to the language it reads, JSON among them, until models trained on Datom and Ethos come.
