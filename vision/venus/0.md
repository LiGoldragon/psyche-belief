# Venus (Śukra)

A painting, not a diagram. Every part of it has the quality of Śukra, and nothing in it has another quality: the subject, the setting, the things, the format, the composition, the light, the colour, the stroke and the surface. It holds no word, no letter, no numeral and no glyph. Where the correspondence below gives two readings of one thing, both are present in the one picture.

## The painting

- Twilight in spring, in a garden pavilion open to a lotus pool: lamps are lit while the sky still holds light, and one bright star stands in it.
- A woman in her youth, lovely, with a broad chest and face, full strong limbs, wide pleasing eyes, beautiful lips, and fine black curling hair. Her skin is dusky, dark as a blade of dūrvā grass, and she is wrapped in a silvery-white radiance; her clothes are of many colours.
- She reclines on a couch among silk cushions, garlanded, wearing diamonds, pearls and silver, and plays a stringed instrument; she is cheerful, affectionate and at play.
- Around her: perfumes and ointments in small vessels, sandalwood, wine, sweets and spices, jewelry, gifts just exchanged; musicians, a dancer, a poet; a lover arriving.
- A peacock and a parrot; a cow at the garden's edge; flowering trees, creepers, an olive, lotuses on the water.

## Format, composition and light

- An upright oval composition of long sinuous curves, every line leading to the next without a break; the whole surface filled and ornamented, no bare place.
- The light is cool and silver from the sky and moist with dew, warm from the lamps on her skin.

## Manner

- Smooth, jewel-like, decorative painting: thin transparent glazes, enamelled colour, pattern on every cloth and leaf, a perfect finish with no stroke showing.
- Colours: silver white, pearl, dusky green-brown skin, and a variegated play of rose, blue, green and gold in the cloth and flowers.

## The correspondence, whole

### nature

Śukra is a benefic, feminine power of attraction, pleasure and refinement. Water and moistening are its firmest natural associations. It brings ease into a life and satisfaction into a relationship. Desire is its moving force: the wish to draw close to a person or a thing and to take pleasure in the meeting. That wish can sustain tenderness, art and hospitality as well as luxury. It is also a power of potency and the passing on of life.

Spring is its season, southeast its quarter and a fortnight its customary measure of time. It is associated with youth, when attraction and taste are taking shape. Smell is especially close to it. Fragrance can bring delight before its source is seen.

**Warming and cold.** In the account of physical powers Venus *θερμαίνει μὲν γὰρ ἠρέμα*, warms moderately because of its proximity to the Sun, and *μάλιστα δὲ ὑγραίνει*, chiefly moistens. In a thermal classification, *mṛdavaśca śitā* gives cold as the property of the gentle group, including Venus.

**Time of strength and sect.** In one strength roster Venus belongs to those strong *niśi*, at night; a general temporal rule gives it strength *ahni*, by day. Night sect, *νυκτερινοὺς*, is affiliation. A third-of-night rule gives *niśārdhe*, the middle of the night, a specified interval. Another interval roster gives “midnight” in its English translation; its Sanskrit wording is unverified here.

**Companions of the Sun.** An invocation calls Mercury and Venus together *fidi Solis comites*, faithful companions of the Sun.

**Assigned taste.** One ordered list gives *āmla*, sour. Another sensory account gives *ἐλλιπωτάτη*, rendered greasy, in its taste clause.

### character

Śukra is affectionate, graceful, cheerful and playful. It notices beauty and knows how to respond to it, whether in a person, an object or a performance. It loves music, dancing and singing, and its own speech can have charm and ease. The love of cleanliness and order gives pleasure a setting where others can share it. In a favorable condition it cares for affection and decorum; in a poor one indulgence can become careless or undiscriminating. Its nearest relationships are the spouse and lover, but also friends, mothers and nurses.

### body

The portrait has a lovely body, broad chest and face, strong or full limbs, and wide, pleasing eyes. Some forms emphasize the weight of the shoulders and thighs; the body is not simply slight. A fine voice belongs beside the beauty of face and lips. Phlegm and wind are the prevailing constitution, and reproductive seed is the characteristic bodily essence. In the human body the neck, face, liver, flesh and reproductive functions fall within its field. A mark on the left side of the face is a more particular association.

### occupations

Musicians, dancers and poets carry its joy into public life. Perfumers, makers of wreaths and ornaments, weavers, dyers and dealers in clothes or fine goods give beauty a material form. The work can be exacting: gold workers spin or shape precious material, and painters and instrument makers can make color or sound usable. Some of the more technical or theatrical trades belong to Venus working with Mercury. There is a courtly side as well: the advisor or minister.

**Musical enjoyment and skilled work.** When Venus rules a nativity, *musicis semper delectationibus* speaks of a person’s delight in music. When Venus both rules the nativity and is placed *in deiectis locis*, in depressed places, the separate result is *organarios pictores*, instrument specialists and painters of bodily likenesses. Enjoyment and profession are separate outcomes.

### things

Perfumes, ointments, flowers worn as garlands, ornaments, jewelry and durable fine cloth come first. Beds and couches, silk, vehicles and stored valuables supply ease. Wine, spices and sweets are things of taste or hospitality. Song, dance and instrumental music may come together in celebration. Gifts passed between people, marriage and sexual union make the same power visible in exchange and intimacy.

### places

The bedroom is the clearest place. It belongs beside the dancing hall, the quarters of a courtesan, a garden or park and other places made for enjoyment. Markets and workshops are where the goods of pleasure or adornment are made and traded. A house can share all these associations on a smaller scale.

### animals and plants

Flowers, flowering trees, the lotus, and creeping or sap-filled plants stand out. Fragrant woods and spices include sandalwood, agarwood, cassia and nutmeg. The peacock and parrot are distinctive associations; the cow and buffalo, and also the horse and elephant, belong alongside them. The olive is another of its plants.

### metals and stones

Diamond, pearl and silver are leading materials. Gems as a class also belong here.

### colours

The human-shaped portrait has fine black curling hair.

**White, dusky and variegated.** A Greek colour clause assigns Venus white, printed *λευνή*. A planetary colour list assigns *sita*, white; a separate portrait calls the figure *rajatāvadātaḥ*, silvery-white. A description of the figure calls it *śukraḥ śyāvas*, dusky Venus; “variegated” is another rendering there, with no verified underlying variant. In a separate account, *vicitra* names a variegated assigned colour, *dūrvādalaśyāmalaḥ* is complexion dark like a dūrvā blade, and *citrāmbaro* identifies multicoloured apparel. A further portrait has *śyāmaḥ śukro*, dusky Venus, alongside *citra*, varied, in its assigned-colour list. One English translation renders the dūrvā complexion yellow-green. Assigned white, a silvery figure, different complexion readings and multicoloured apparel are separately sourced; the garment does not settle the assigned hue.

### illnesses

Śukra, acting alone favorably, is linked with bodily health. When well-placed benefics prevail over harmful planets, it can moderate illness or make a blemish less disfiguring; the relief is conditional. Its capacity to sustain bodily life is also what makes reproductive difficulty a distinct adverse theme. In a woman’s nativity, Venus in the sixth place is linked to danger in childbirth or repeated miscarriage. These are outcomes concerning a person in a stated configuration, not properties of the figure.
