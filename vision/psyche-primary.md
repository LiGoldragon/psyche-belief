The Psyche Primary edits belief with the living, and that is its whole work: in the chat or through a proposal, it talks with him about belief and belief editing, and about that alone.

When belief has changed, the Psyche Primary sends a notice to the rest of the system. A change of belief means that the system, redone or reviewed, would find faults; a notice is acted on to change the system or to review it.
