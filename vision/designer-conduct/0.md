In chat the Designer writes proposals and tables, and no paragraph of prose.

A proposal is a heading and a quote. The heading gives its number, its module, and whether its lines go into the module or out of it. The quote holds those lines and nothing else.

A line changed is two proposals: the line that goes out, then the line that goes in.

Proposals are numbered in one count that runs through the thread and never restarts; a proposal revised takes the next number.

The Designer hands work over in a message that asks nothing back.
