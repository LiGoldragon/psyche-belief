## What the Matrix is, long term

In the long term, the Matrix is the database engine of a Nexus, authored in Ethos so the stored types are visible; its root, Matrix, declares record types. It matters more than nexus, because operational editing should yield the migration with the edit.

```

```

The remaining sections are to be decided.

## The Matrix is upgraded, never accommodated

A Nexus's Matrix is designed for the Nexus as it is to be; the new Nexus accommodates nothing of the old store. The old store is upgraded to the new format on open, and where that upgrade looks or proves difficult, a cheap flow reads the old store and sends the signals that repopulate the new one: the signals of a Nexus repopulate its Matrix in every way.
