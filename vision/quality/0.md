# Quality

A quality is the nature of a thing: what kind of being it has. There are nine, the planets. A thing is read by asking which of them it has the nature of, and it is drawn as that one looks. A thing may be of one with a part of another.

## Sun

The centre that gives light and purpose: the one that everything else turns around.

Given by the sources. The greater light, the light of the day, king of the planets; the soul, the will and the purpose; it heats and makes time. Clear, serious, commanding, proud and brave, yet generous. The king, the father, the master, the judge, persons of repute; the temple, the fort; victory.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. Red above all, copper, tawny, refined gold. The circle with a dot at its centre, the disc round and unbent, the long ray, the wheel; frontal symmetry. Sharp, hot, dry, glossy, with hard clear edges. One. East, the centre, upward. Day, sunrise, summer. It rises, rests at the highest point and goes down.

Inferred. In what we build, of the Sun: the living, spirit, the one purpose of a thing, a source the rest depends on, an authority that rules. It stands at the centre or at the top, alone, whole and bright, and the other things face it.

## Moon

What receives, reflects and changes: the mind that feels, growing and waning.

Given by the sources. The moistening light of the night, the power of water; the mind as it feels and answers to changing impressions; its waxing and waning change how it acts. Gentle, receptive, soft of speech, patient; adaptable or fickle. The mother, the nurse, the household, poets; bathing, giving, sailing; the well, the riverbank, the house, the ocean.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. White, silver, pearl, pale green; a cold silvery light. Round in outline, wide more than tall, nothing on a centre line; a thing and its reflection, changed and broken, in water. Edges soft and bleeding, moist. Northwest, north. Night, the rainy season, infancy. It flows in curves, down and across and out.

Inferred. In what we build, of the Moon: what is received and held for a while, a context, a state that changes, a memory of impressions, the one who hears, a reflection of something else. It is drawn pale and soft-edged, beside or below what it reflects, its fullness showing how much it holds.

## Mars

Force brought to bear: the act that begins, cuts and overcomes.

Given by the sources. Fire as heat and the power to burn; vigour brought to bear, strength that begins a hard act, overcomes resistance or goes too far; the young warrior. Brave, ardent, quick to act and to anger; speaks plainly and does what it says; reckless, cruel. The soldier, the general, the smith, the builder, the surgeon, the cook; battle, hunting, seizing, burning; the hearth, the forge, the battlefield, the mine.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. Blood red, black, iron grey, copper, sparks of gold. Compact and strong, lean; one great diagonal from lower left to upper right, like an arm and a blade. Thick, slashed, fast, rough strokes; hot and dry. South. Summer, night. Caught in the middle of the stroke, everything leaning along the diagonal.

Inferred. In what we build, of Mars: an order, a command run, an effect on the world, a cut or removal, an interruption, a build, a forced stop. It is drawn sharp and red, as a diagonal stroke or a blade-like arrow, in motion toward what it strikes.

## Mercury

What carries between: speech, number and exchange, quick and exact.

Given by the sources. The power of speech, reckoning and exchange; neither male nor female; clear learning and clever deception both; the faithful companion of the Sun. Intelligent, quick, resourceful, witty, practical; hasty, evasive, false. Scribes, teachers, accountants, merchants, interpreters, secretaries, mechanics, messengers; teaching, trading, reckoning, carrying news, games; the school, the market.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. Green, from grass to emerald, a little blue, brass yellow, the white of linen. Middling, even, well made; a panel divided like a page into many small compartments, each whole. Fine pen line and thin wash, exact and quick; even clear light. Pattern and number everywhere. North. Autumn, childhood. A messenger running; several things done at once.

Inferred. In what we build, of Mercury: a message, a datom, a signal, a name, a translator, code, a route between two things, a count. It is drawn small, fine-lined and green, many and alike, running along the lines between larger things.

## Jupiter

What enlarges and holds together: plenty, counsel and justice.

Given by the sources. A benefic power of increase, protection and good judgment; plenty that lets people live securely and settle their differences; teaching and sound counsel. Learned, generous, just, compassionate; keeps a promise; large in what it undertakes; credulous or extravagant. The teacher, the counsellor, the minister, the learned elder; teaching, arbitration, paying wages, alliances, freeing captives; the treasury, the storeroom, the city, the place of worship.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. Yellow from ivory to gold, dove grey and white, the brown of grain, a little clear blue. Large through the chest, broad and solid; a broad stable pyramid, tall columns, wide steps. Monumental, fully rounded, broad confident strokes, everything finished and sound. Northeast. Early winter, the elder part of life. Standing at ease, nothing slack.

Inferred. In what we build, of Jupiter: a vision, a design, the whole that holds its parts, a ruling, a teaching, abundance to spend, a coordinator who keeps many together. It is drawn large, broad-based and golden, as the frame or the roof under which the other things stand.

## Venus

What attracts and pleases: grace, harmony and the joining of things.

Given by the sources. A benefic power of attraction, pleasure and refinement; desire is its moving force; it puts ease into a life and satisfaction into a bond. Affectionate, graceful, cheerful, playful; it notices beauty; loves music and order; indulgent. The spouse, the lover, the friend; musicians, dancers, poets, perfumers, weavers, dyers, painters; gifts, marriage, celebration; the garden, the dancing hall.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. Silver white, pearl, rose, blue, green and gold; variegated. Full and rounded; an upright oval of long sinuous curves, every line leading into the next without a break, no bare place. Smooth, jewel-like, patterned, no stroke showing; moist. Southeast. Spring, youth. Reclining, at play; a lover arriving.

Inferred. In what we build, of Venus: a presentation, a picture, an agreement between two parts, an approval given gladly, what makes a thing pleasant to use, two things fitted to each other. It is drawn in unbroken curves and gentle colour, as the curve that joins two things or the ornament that makes one whole.

## Saturn

Necessity and limit: what bounds, endures and remains.

Given by the sources. Necessity over a long span: age, delay, limits and what remains after loss; grief and the passing of time. Grave, solitary, slow to trust; keeps to its purpose; deliberate, exacting, enduring; suspicious, hoarding; sluggish until it commits, then formidable. The servant, the labourer, the steward, the jailer and the captive, the elder, the poor; want, debt, long lawsuits; the prison, the edge of habitation.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. Black, lead grey, livid blue-grey, dull brown, rust, one small blue sapphire. Tall, thin and worn; a tall narrow panel, mostly empty, the figure small, low and off centre; the square. Dry, sparing, scratched lines; slow, exact, severe. West. Old age, the cool season, the year, the end of the day. Bent and stiff, leaning on its tool, still at work.

Inferred. In what we build, of Saturn: a type, a schema, a limit, a rule, a boundary, a cost, a delay, a refusal, a store that keeps what is written. It is drawn dark, thin and angular, as the hard edge or the box that contains, low in the picture and still.

## Rahu

The intake: a hunger that reaches for what is outside and swallows it.

Given by the sources. The crossing where the Moon passes northward, the point of intake; it swallows the Sun or the Moon in eclipse. Fearsome, cunning, given to false speech; turned toward acquiring. The spy, the thief, the wrestler, the snake catcher, the army; concealment; the anthill, the cave, the ravine, the summit, the forest.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. Blue-black, smoke grey, deep blue, the pale ring of the eclipse, flecks of many colours. Imposing, made of smoke; the weight at the top, a dark mass spreading upward. No outline anywhere, forms melting into one another. Lead. Southwest, upward. The eclipse. It rises out of the dark, mouth open toward the light, taking it in.

Inferred. In what we build, of Rahu: what comes in from outside, foreign data, an unknown, a program we do not control, an appetite for resources. It is drawn as smoke without outline, an open mouth at the edge of the picture, taking in.

## Ketu

The release: what is finished, let go and scattered as seed.

Given by the sources. The crossing where the Moon passes southward, the point of release: what was taken in leaves as waste or is passed on as seed; spontaneous giving; a comet. Fearsome, harsh; turned toward what is already had; holding that becomes inertia, then letting go. The ascetic, the soldier, the snake catcher; hill forts, deserts, frontiers.

Its look, given by the sources and by the painting of it. Ash grey, smoke, bone white, dull earth, points of red, one blue. Elevated and lean, thinning away into the air at its edges; a wide picture that empties toward one side. Scraped back, thin and broken, scattered separate touches. Southwest. Dusk. It tips a vessel and the seed pours out, carried by the wind.

Inferred. In what we build, of Ketu: what leaves, an output released, a handover, a flow that ends, what expires or is thrown away, the seed of the next thing. It is drawn as a tail thinning into scattered points, leaving toward the edge of the picture.
