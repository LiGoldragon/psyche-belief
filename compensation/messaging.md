---
description: A flow is about to send, relay, or answer a message from another flow.
dependencies: [vision-messaging]
---

A reply that only acknowledges is not sent: delivered, carried, nothing owed, no action.

Words attributed to the living are relayed with the record file and heading that hold them, or not relayed.

A topic crosses between aspects in one hop each way; a relay of a relay is refused, and the sender is pointed to the record.

A queue belongs to a metaflow, never to a flow: what arrives while a flow is refreshed or replaced waits and is delivered whole to its successor; a queue is closed only when its metaflow is retired.

A seat whose restart is ordered is sent nothing, the order included: the restart is done from outside, or the seat is left to restart itself at its rest; a message typed into it is a wake.

A question already answered by a message in flight is not re-asked; the asker reads the answer.

Each hour a flow asks whether its last hour moved a file, a book or a deployment; an hour that only produced messages is reported as such to its Primary.

Every message a flow sends opens with the line "From <Aspect> <topic> <Layer> <FLOW_ID>, a machine:".

A prompt that opens with a "From …, a machine:" line, names a sender, or carries a datom head or a record path is a machine's message; its words are logged as the living's only from the record it names.
