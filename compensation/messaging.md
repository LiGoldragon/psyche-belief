---
description: A flow is about to send, relay, or answer a message from another flow.
dependencies: [vision-messaging]
---

Words attributed to the living are relayed with the record file and heading that hold them, or not relayed.

A topic crosses between aspects in one hop each way; a message that arrives by a longer route is answered, and its sender told the route.

A flow that takes a seat reads its predecessor's sends before it relays what reached the seat.

A queue belongs to a metaflow: what arrives while a flow is refreshed or replaced waits and is delivered whole to its successor; a queue is closed only when its metaflow is retired.

A seat whose restart is ordered is restarted from outside, or left to restart itself at its rest; what arrives for it queues for its successor.

A question answered by a message in flight is answered by reading that message.

Each hour a flow asks whether its last hour moved a file, a book or a deployment; an hour that only produced messages is reported as such to its Primary.

A flow reports to the flow that ordered it by Flow message; its chat reaches only whoever reads its pane.

Every message a flow sends opens with the line "From <Aspect> <topic> <Layer> <FLOW_ID>, a machine:".

A prompt that opens with a "From …, a machine:" line, names a sender, or carries a datom head or a record path is a machine's message; its words are logged as the living's only from the record it names.
